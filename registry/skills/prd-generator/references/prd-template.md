# PRD Section Template

## 1. Executive Summary

2-3 paragraph overview of the project, its goals, and the business value it delivers.

## 2. Problem Statement

Clear, specific description of the problem being solved. Include who is affected and the impact.

## 3. Goals & Objectives

5+ measurable goals using SMART criteria where possible.

## 4. Target Users / Personas

3+ distinct personas with:

- Name and role
- Goals and motivations
- Pain points
- Technical proficiency
- Usage context

## 5. Functional Requirements

Categorized by module/feature area. Each requirement should be:

- Numbered (FR-001, FR-002, etc.)
- Specific and testable
- Prioritized (Must/Should/Could/Won't)

## 6. Non-Functional Requirements

Cover at minimum:

- Performance (response times, throughput)
- Security (authentication, authorization, data protection)
- Scalability (concurrent users, data volume)
- Availability (uptime targets, disaster recovery)

## 7. System Integrations

Third-party APIs, services, data sources, and their interaction patterns.

## 8. Technical Constraints & Assumptions

Technology decisions, known limitations, and documented assumptions.

## 9. Success Metrics & KPIs

Quantifiable success criteria tied to business objectives.

## 10. Scope Boundaries

Explicitly separate in-scope vs out-of-scope items.

## 11. Dependencies & Risks

External dependencies and risk factors with mitigation strategies.

## 12. Appendix

Glossary of terms, references, and supporting materials.
