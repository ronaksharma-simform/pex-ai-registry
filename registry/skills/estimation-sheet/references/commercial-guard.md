# Commercial Qualification Guard

## Minimum Effective Rate

Ensure project estimates protect minimum effective hourly rates.

## Qualification Checks

1. **Scope clarity:** Is the scope well-defined enough to estimate?
2. **Budget alignment:** Does the client budget support the estimated effort?
3. **Timeline feasibility:** Can the work be completed in the requested timeframe?
4. **Team availability:** Are the required skill sets available?
5. **Risk tolerance:** Are risks manageable within the budget?

## Red Flags

- Scope described in less than 100 words
- No budget range provided
- Timeline is less than 50% of estimated effort
- Requires technologies the team hasn't used before
- Multiple third-party integrations with no API documentation
