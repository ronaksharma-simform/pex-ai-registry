# Sales Call Scoring Rubric

## Individual Scoring Dimensions (each rated 1-10)

### 1. Clarity (Weight: 15%)

- **9-10**: Explanations are crisp, jargon-free when speaking to non-technical audience, well-structured
- **7-8**: Generally clear with minor instances of ambiguity
- **5-6**: Some explanations are confusing or overly complex
- **3-4**: Frequently unclear, uses unexplained jargon
- **1-2**: Consistently confusing, listener would struggle to follow

### 2. Technical Depth (Weight: 15%)

- **9-10**: Demonstrates deep expertise, provides specific technical solutions, references relevant technologies accurately
- **7-8**: Good technical knowledge with minor gaps
- **5-6**: Surface-level technical responses, lacks specificity
- **3-4**: Noticeable technical gaps, some inaccuracies
- **1-2**: Unable to answer technical questions adequately

### 3. Relevance (Weight: 15%)

- **9-10**: Every response directly addresses customer's question/concern, no tangents
- **7-8**: Mostly relevant with minor digressions
- **5-6**: Some responses miss the point or go off-topic
- **3-4**: Frequently off-topic or provides irrelevant information
- **1-2**: Consistently fails to address what was asked

### 4. Professionalism (Weight: 10%)

- **9-10**: Courteous, respectful, appropriate tone throughout
- **7-8**: Professional with minor lapses
- **5-6**: Generally professional but some inappropriate moments
- **3-4**: Several unprofessional moments
- **1-2**: Consistently unprofessional

### 5. Listening Skills (Weight: 15%)

- **9-10**: Actively listens, references customer's earlier points, never interrupts inappropriately
- **7-8**: Good listening with occasional misses
- **5-6**: Sometimes ignores or misinterprets customer input
- **3-4**: Frequently talks over or ignores customer
- **1-2**: Clearly not listening, repeats answered questions

### 6. Empathy & Engagement (Weight: 15%)

- **9-10**: Shows genuine understanding of customer's challenges, builds rapport, asks thoughtful follow-ups
- **7-8**: Generally empathetic with good engagement
- **5-6**: Transactional interaction, limited rapport building
- **3-4**: Disconnected from customer's concerns
- **1-2**: Cold or dismissive

### 7. Problem-Solution Mapping (Weight: 15%)

- **9-10**: Every solution is clearly mapped to a specific customer problem, with rationale
- **7-8**: Good mapping with minor gaps
- **5-6**: Some solutions proposed without clear connection to problems
- **3-4**: Frequently proposes solutions that don't address stated needs
- **1-2**: No attempt to map solutions to problems

## Individual Score Calculation

```
Score = (Clarity * 0.15 + TechDepth * 0.15 + Relevance * 0.15 +
         Professionalism * 0.10 + Listening * 0.15 +
         Empathy * 0.15 + ProblemSolution * 0.15) * 10
```

## Overall Simform Score (0-100)

### Component Breakdown (each 0-25)

#### Preparation & Structure (25 points)

- Was the call agenda clear? (0-5)
- Did the team demonstrate knowledge of the client's business? (0-5)
- Was the presentation/discussion logically structured? (0-5)
- Were relevant case studies or examples referenced? (0-5)
- Was there a clear opening and closing? (0-5)

#### Technical Competence (25 points)

- Were technical questions answered accurately? (0-7)
- Was the proposed solution technically sound? (0-7)
- Were trade-offs and alternatives discussed? (0-6)
- Were limitations honestly communicated? (0-5)

#### Customer Engagement (25 points)

- Was the customer actively involved in the discussion? (0-7)
- Were customer concerns acknowledged and addressed? (0-7)
- Was there good rapport and chemistry? (0-6)
- Did the team ask insightful discovery questions? (0-5)

#### Outcomes & Next Steps (25 points)

- Were concrete next steps agreed upon? (0-7)
- Were timelines discussed? (0-6)
- Were responsibilities assigned? (0-6)
- Was there a clear call-to-action? (0-6)

## Score Interpretation

- **90-100**: Exceptional — Best-in-class sales performance
- **80-89**: Strong — Professional and effective, minor improvements possible
- **70-79**: Good — Solid performance with clear areas for growth
- **60-69**: Average — Meets basic expectations but significant room for improvement
- **50-59**: Below Average — Multiple areas need attention
- **Below 50**: Poor — Requires immediate coaching intervention
